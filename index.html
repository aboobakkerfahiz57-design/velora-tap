About VELORA TAP

Your Business Deserves a Smarter Identity.

VELORA TAP is a digital identity platform designed to simplify the way businesses connect with people.

Through NFC-enabled cards and QR technology, businesses can share their digital profiles, contact information, social media and important links in a convenient way.

We bring together smart physical products and digital business experiences to help brands build meaningful connections.
