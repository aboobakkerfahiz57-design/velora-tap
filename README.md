# VELORA TAP

**Smart NFC & QR-Powered Business Identity Platform**

VELORA TAP is a digital business platform that connects customers with businesses through NFC technology and QR codes.

Customers can tap an NFC-enabled display or scan a QR code to open a business profile and access its important links.

## Features

- Custom business profile pages
- NFC and QR code integration
- Google Reviews links
- WhatsApp contact links
- Instagram links
- Website links
- Business logo uploads
- Admin authentication
- Business profile management
- Activate and deactivate business profiles

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Supabase
- GitHub
- Vercel (planned deployment)

## Database

Supabase is used for:

- Business information
- Admin profiles
- Authentication
- Business logo storage
- Business event records

## Business Profile

Each business receives a unique profile slug.

Example:

`business.html?slug=abc-restaurant`
